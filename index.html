About Apex Horizon Academy

Cultivating intellectual curiosity, ethical character, and innovative problem solvers ready to lead the future.

Our Mission

To deliver an invigorating, research-backed learning atmosphere that empowers diverse young minds with critical inquiry, scientific discovery, and profound compassion.

Our Vision

To be the global benchmark for 21st-century educational excellence, blending classical humanist inquiry with artificial intelligence, applied robotics, and sustainability.

Core Values

Integrity, Collaborative Innovation, Rigor, Empathy, and Resilience. We instil an unwavering commitment to societal upliftment and personal mastery in every scholar.

Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Find rapid answers to essential questions regarding admissions, transport, scholarships, and residential life.

Admission to Apex Horizon Academy is based on holistic assessment: past academic records from preceding two years, cognitive diagnostic assessment in mathematics and language, and an interactive meeting with our admissions dean.

Tuition fees are structured in four convenient quarterly installments. Complete transparent fee schedules including laboratory dues, books, and technology subscriptions are downloadable in our official prospectus.

All academy buses are air-conditioned, speed-governed, and fitted with high-definition CCTV and real-time GPS tracking accessible to parents via the Parent Portal App. A trained female conductor accompanies each transit route.

We provide a dual-pathway curriculum allowing students to choose between recognized national secondary boards and international certification frameworks, supported by dedicated honors and AP modules.

Yes. The Apex Horizon Foundation awards up to 100% tuition scholarships for exceptional academic merit, national-level sports champions, and innovation leaders through our Annual Horizon Fellowship examination.

Our students wear a tailored formal navy blazer, crisp white shirt, and academy tie for formal assemblies, alongside an athletic sports kit for games days. Uniforms can be tailored via our on-campus boutique or through our online store.
